Why NexaHRM

HR work should not run on spreadsheets and chat messages

These are the everyday problems NexaHRM is designed to take off your plate.

📁

Employee details are scattered

Profiles, documents and contacts sit in different files and folders. Finding one record takes far too long.

💬

Attendance and leave run on chat

Leave requests arrive on WhatsApp, approvals get lost and balances are tracked by hand.

🔁

HR time goes to repetitive admin

Answering the same questions and compiling the same reports leaves little time for people-focused work.

✨

NexaHRM puts every employee, request and approval in one place, so HR spends less time chasing and more time helping people.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is NexaHRM?
NexaHRM is human resource management software that brings employee records, attendance, leave, onboarding and reports into one system.
Who is it for?
Growing companies and HR teams that currently manage people data with spreadsheets, files and chat messages.
Does it include payroll?
The first focus is employee records, attendance and leave. Payroll is planned for a later phase once the core is solid.
When will it be available?
NexaHRM is in development and there is no fixed launch date yet. Early-access members will be contacted first.
How do I get early access?
Use the "Get early access" button on this page. It opens an email to me with a few questions about your company, and I will reply when early access opens.
Is employee data kept private?
Privacy and role-based access are core design goals, so each person only sees the information that belongs to their role.
